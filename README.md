# Management Comment Tracker

Automate monthly GM commentary by finding missed manager comments on Ticket Refunds, Cashier Differences (MEMO), Relationship Vouchers and Sorry Vouchers — so the management team can be held accountable.

## Open the tracker

**https://coreyleerobbins.github.io/management-comment-tracker/**

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Bookmark that link; it always opens the newest version.

## Where your data lives

Nothing you upload or enter is stored in this repository.

- The tracker saves automatically in your browser, per company and site.
- **Link Data File** writes the same data to a `.json` file on your computer or a shared drive, so it survives a cleared browser or a new PC.
- **First time on the GitHub link?** Your browser treats it as a new site, so click **Open Data File** once and pick your existing `.json` data file. Everything comes back.

## Fixing a past month

Open the month from the **Viewing** chips on the Results page (or set the **Reporting Period** on Configure), then use **Edit Review & MEMO Queue** to change assignments or Pass/Fail, or **Re-upload This Month** to replace that month's files. Other months aren't touched and YTD totals recalculate.
